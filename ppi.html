Why You Need a PPI

Most hidden faults don't appear during a short test drive. Buying without an independent check can cost millions in repairs. Our inspection helps uncover:

  • Accident damage & structural repairs
  • Flood damage and hidden rust
  • Engine performance problems
  • Transmission faults & clutch wear
  • Suspension wear & steering play
  • Brake system defects
  • Electrical issues & blown modules
  • ECU fault codes & hidden dashboard warnings
  • Odometer inconsistencies
  • Poor previous repairs and makeshift fixes

What We Inspect

Our comprehensive checklist leaves no stone unturned across all major vehicle zones:

Exterior

  • Paint condition & thickness
  • Body alignment & panel gaps
  • Accident repairs & frame integrity
  • Rust & corrosion check
  • Glass, lights, wheels, and tyres

Interior

  • Dashboard & control functionality
  • Seats, upholstery, and seat belts
  • Air conditioning & climate control
  • Steering controls & electronics
  • Airbag presence & deployment check

Engine

  • Oil and fluid leaks check
  • Engine mounts & stability
  • Cooling system & radiator health
  • Drive belts, hoses, and battery
  • Charging system performance

Transmission & Drivetrain

  • Automatic & manual transmission operation
  • Clutch feel and engagement
  • Differential & transfer case condition
  • Driveshafts, CV joints, and boots

Suspension & Brakes

  • Shock absorbers & struts
  • Bushings, control arms, and ball joints
  • Steering rack and wheel bearings
  • Brake pads, discs, lines, and fluid condition
  • ABS operation and response

Computer Diagnostics

This is where Dreamland stands out. We perform professional electronic diagnostics to check:

  • Engine ECU, ABS, Airbag, & Transmission modules
  • Hybrid system health (where applicable)
  • Live sensor data streams
  • Stored, intermittent, and pending fault codes

Road Test Evaluation

We test-drive the vehicle (where safely permissible) to evaluate real-world engine performance, gear shifting smoothness, steering responsiveness, braking efficiency, suspension behavior, acceleration, and abnormal noises.
