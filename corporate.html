Request Corporate Proposal

Corporate FleetCare Packages

Structured maintenance plans designed to maximize vehicle uptime, prevent sudden breakdowns, and control operating costs.

Essential FleetCare

For small businesses and organizations operating local fleets.

  • Scheduled preventive maintenance
  • Routine vehicle inspections
  • Computer diagnostics
  • Basic electrical diagnosis
  • Brake and suspension checks
  • Service reminders & records
  • Priority workshop scheduling

Professional FleetCare

For companies with a growing fleet requiring deeper insights.

  • Everything in Essential, plus:
  • Advanced diagnostics
  • Live-data analysis
  • Comprehensive periodic inspections
  • Fault investigation & root-cause diagnosis
  • Digital vehicle history & reporting
  • Priority breakdown support

Enterprise FleetCare

For organizations that depend heavily on continuous fleet mobility.

  • Everything in Professional, plus:
  • Dedicated fleet maintenance program
  • Periodic fleet health assessments
  • Fleet-wide diagnostic analysis
  • Maintenance forecasting
  • Monthly management reports
  • Corporate account & SLA management

Custom Fleet Contract
